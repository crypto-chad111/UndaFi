# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful.

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
- Do not ask for help using UndaFi to watch people without their knowledge.
  It is for your own home.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
